# Conversation Flows. MAA Intelligent Chatbot

These are the conversation types tested in the MAA chatbot.
### 1. Greeting
**User:** Hi
**Bot:** Hello! MAA welcomes you. Shows the main things MAA can help with.

### 2. About MAA
**User:** What is MAA?
**Bot:** MAA gives an introduction to MAA and explains its purpose.

### 3. Services
**User:** What services are available?
**Bot:** MAA lists Food, Medicine, Travel Talk to MAA, emergency support and account features.

### 4. Food
**User:** Tell me about home made food.
**Bot:** MAA gives information about cooks, menus, filters and ratings.

### 5. Medicine

**User:** How do I order medicine?
**Bot:** MAA explains the Medicine section, including ordering, reminders and health tips.

### 6. Navigation

**User:** Where can I find registration?
**Bot:** MAA guides the user to the Get Started/Login option.

### 7. Emergency

**User:** What if I need emergency help while traveling?
**Bot:** MAA provides the emergency numbers and emergency travel information.

### 8. Report a Problem

**User:** I have a problem with an order.
**Bot:** MAA guides the user to describe the issue and contact support.

### 9. Out-of-Scope Question

**User:** Who is Elon Musk?
**Bot:** MAA politely explains that the question is, outside MAAs scope.

### 10. Follow-up Question

**User:** Tell me about travel assistance.
**Bot:** MAA gives travel information.
**User:** Fees?
**Bot:** MAA uses the travel topic to understand the follow-up. If no fee information exists MAA does not make up a price.
