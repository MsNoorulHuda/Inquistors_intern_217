# User Guide — MAA Intelligent Chatbot

## Opening the chatbot
1. Start the backend (see `installation.md`).
2. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8001` in a browser.
3. You will see the MAA chat panel with a welcome message and three suggested
   questions.

## Asking questions
Type a question about MAA into the message box at the bottom and press the send
button (↑). You can ask about:
- What MAA is and its purpose
- Home Made Food (cooks, menus, filters)
- Medicine and Health (ordering, reminders, health tips)
- Travel Assistance (trip planning, popular destinations, emergency travel)
- Emergency numbers (Police, Ambulance, Women Helpline, Tourist Helpline)
- Talk to MAA (the AI companion)
- Registration, login, and navigation
- Reporting a problem or contacting support

## Reading an answer
Each answer from the knowledge base shows small "source chips" underneath it  these
name the internal knowledge file the answer came from, so you can verify where the
information is coming from.

## When the bot doesn't know something
If your question is not covered in the MAA knowledge base, the bot will say so clearly
instead of guessing, and suggest contacting MAA support.

## Starting a new conversation
Click "New chat" in the header to reset the conversation and clear the follow-up memory.
